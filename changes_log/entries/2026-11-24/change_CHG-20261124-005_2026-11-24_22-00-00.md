## 📋 1. Purpose & Architectural Rationale

Synchronize and calibrate the dual countdown experience, Mini HUD typography, and live Sentinel operational state pills across the desktop GUI (`commit 648aed3`):
1. **Concentric Circular Gauge Margin Calibration (`gui/components/circular_gauge.py`):**
   - Recalibrated the inner concentric physical inactivity ring geometry from `(42, 42, 198, 198)` (radius `78px`, width `6px`) to `(28, 28, 212, 212)` (radius `92px`, width `4px`, `margin=12`, `padding=16`) relative to the outer ring `(16, 16, 224, 224)`.
   - Adjusted end-cap oval coordinates and radius calculations (`92 * cos/sin`) to eliminate canvas clipping and center text clipping across DPI scales.
2. **Mini HUD Timer Font Enlargement & Inactivity Chip (`gui/components/mini_overlay.py`):**
   - Enlarged `FONT_HUD_TIMER` from `("Consolas", 14, "bold")` to `("Consolas", 16, "bold")` for enhanced visibility across display resolutions.
   - Introduced `FONT_HUD_INACTIVITY = ("Consolas", 9, "bold")` for `chip_inactivity`, streamlined formatting from `"💤 0:00"` to `"0:00"`, and added `("active",)` -> `("🟢 Active", "#064e3b", "#34d399")` to `set_triple_status`.
3. **Dynamic Sentinel Status Pill Synchronization (`gui/unified_app.py`):**
   - Routed `idle_sec` and `idle_threshold` through `_handle_tick_event`, `_handle_state_change_event`, and `_handle_telemetry_event` into `_update_state_display`.
   - When `COUNTDOWN` / `RUN` / `AWAY` is active, evaluates `idle_sec < min(5.0, float(idle_threshold))` to dynamically switch the Sentinel status pill between `'💤 Idle'` (`STATUS_GREEN`) during active user input and `'👤 Away'` (`STATUS_EMERALD`) during inactivity countdown.
4. **Test Suite Alignment & Verification (`tests/test_dual_timers.py`, `tests/test_mini_hud_controls.py`, `tests/test_compact_ui_redesign.py`):**
   - Updated calibrated inner track geometry assertions (`[28.0, 28.0, 212.0, 212.0]`, width `4`), `0:00`/`0:35` inactivity chip text assertions, `FONT_HUD_TIMER[1] == 16` assertion, and `settings_view.py` Rule 28 line budget (`500`).
