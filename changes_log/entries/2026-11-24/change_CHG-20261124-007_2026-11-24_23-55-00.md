## 📋 1. Purpose & Architectural Rationale

Resolve dynamic status pill / badge synchronization failure across Full Window and Mini HUD modes:
1. **Root Cause Resolution:**
   - Fixed regression where event handlers in `gui/unified_app.py` bypassed state updates.
   - Restored and centralized pure UI status mapping into `gui/components/status_resolver.py`.
   - Prevented premature matching of `SHUTDOWN` over `PRE_SHUTDOWN_GRACE` / `CLOSING`.
   - Guaranteed `[ ⏸️ Paused ]` state persistence without clobbering by subsequent countdown ticks.

2. **User Clarification Architectural Integration:**
   - Brief input (<5s) displays `[ 💤 Idle ]` and reverts to `[ 👤 Away ]` during countdown.
   - Distinct phase pills: `[ 🔍 Evaluating ]` for `AI_EVALUATION`, `[ 💬 Waiting ]` for `WAITING_USER_PROMPT`, and `[ 🛑 Blocked ]` for process blockers.
3. **Universal Rule 28 Modularity Compliance:**
